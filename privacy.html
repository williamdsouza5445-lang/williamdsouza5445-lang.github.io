Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

This privacy policy applies to the personal YouTube automation tool maintained by William D'Souza ("Application"). The Application is designed solely for personal management and scheduled publishing of video content.

Data We Access

The Application accesses the following data through authorized Google APIs:

  • Google Drive Files: Video and metadata files located in dedicated user folders to prepare uploads.
  • YouTube Channel Data: Channel information and upload endpoints necessary to publish scheduled videos and monitor publish status.

How We Use Data

The data accessed by the Application is strictly used to upload and schedule the owner's own video assets to their own YouTube channel. We do not store personal data on external commercial databases or servers beyond what is necessary to run the automated upload workflows.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

The Application does not sell, rent, disclose, or share any user data with third parties. No third-party tracking or advertising SDKs are employed.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke the Application's access permissions at any time via Google Account Settings at:
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
